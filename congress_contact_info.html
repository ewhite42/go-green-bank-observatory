Congressional Contact Info

Thank you for Contacting Congress!

Calling and/or emailing works best. Members to contact in order of potential effectiveness are as follows:
  • WV Senators and House Representatives
  • Senate CJS (oversees NSF Budget) Appropriations Subcommittee Members
  • Senate Appropriations Committee Chair and Vice Chair/Ranking Member
  • House CJS (oversees NSF Budget) Appropriations Subcommittee Members
  • House Appropriations Chair and Vice Chair/Ranking Member
  • Senate Science Subcommittee Members & Head Committee Key Members
  • House Science Subcommittee Members & Head Committee Key Members
  • Your Congress Members
  • Other Stakeholders
Please note that if you do not live in the state of a Congress person you want to email – you may not be able to email them directly, but you can call and ask their staff for a way to fax a comment or email them separately. It is best to call, email, and/or fax them as mailed letters can take up to 4 weeks to get to them due to security screenings.

Congressional Appropriations Committees

Senate

The Senate Appropriations Committee is the largest committee in the U.S. Senate, consisting of 29 members, and writes the legislation that allocates federal funds (i.e. the overall budget) to the numerous government agencies, departments, and organizations on an annual basis.

Subcommittees are tasked with drafting legislation to allocate funds to government agencies within their jurisdictions (i.e. makes the budgets for agencies like the NSF). These subcommittees are responsible for reviewing the President’s budget request, hearing testimony from government officials and other witnesses, and drafting the spending plans for the coming fiscal year. Their work is passed on to the full Senate Appropriations Committee, which may review and modify the bills and approve them for consideration by the full Senate. [From: https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/about/jurisdiction]

The Senate Appropriations Subcommittee for Commerce, Justice, Science, and Related Agencies decides the budgets of the following agencies which provide critical investments for scientific research at the:
  • National Science Foundation (NSF)
  • National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)
  • National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration(NOAA)
  • National Institute for Standards and Technology (NIST)
  • Economic Development Administration
  • and more
We have learned in the past that contacting the members of the Senate Commerce Justice, Science, and Related Agencies Appropriations Subcommittee is very helpful as they will directly influence the NSF’s budget.

Also, it would be good to contact the main Senate Appropriations Committee Chair, Vice Chair/Ranking Member, and WV Senator on this committee. Please find their contact information below.

We have learned in the past that contacting the members of the House Commerce Justice, Science, and Related Agencies Appropriations Subcommittee is very helpful as they will directly influence the NSF’s budget.
